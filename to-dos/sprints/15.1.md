# S-AUTH-15.1: Sovereign encrypted vault container (Age / Argon2id)

Status: Planned; task IDs are allocated in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Goal and Scope

Implement the Age-encrypted backup and restore engine in `src/authy_local/backup.py`, encrypting multi-vault credentials with Argon2id-derived keys into standard `.age` files.

Non-goals: Unencrypted JSON or CSV exports by default.

## Entry Conditions and Dependencies

Dependencies: Phase 14 completed; `pyrage` or pure-Python Age cryptographic primitives.
