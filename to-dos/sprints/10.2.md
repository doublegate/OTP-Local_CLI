# S-AUTH-10.2: KeePassXC KDBX4 database direct engine and unified multi-vault resolver

Status: Planned; task IDs are allocated in [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md).

## Goal and Scope

Implement direct native reading and writing of KeePassXC `.kdbx4` encrypted database files in `src/authy_local/backends/keepass.py` (supporting composite master passwords, keyfiles, and hardware challenge-response credentials), coupled with a federated multi-vault resolver in `src/authy_local/federation.py` aggregating accounts across 1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass, GPG, TPM, and Keychain.

Non-goals: Modifying third-party KDBX schema definitions; cloud synchronizations.

## Entry Conditions and Dependencies

Dependencies: S-AUTH-10.1 (Bitwarden backend operational).
